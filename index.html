QUESTIONS

Before you call.

A few answers to the questions that come up most often.

How do I start a matter? +

Call the direct line during office hours or submit the case assessment form below. We will discuss your timeline, what is in front of you, and the immediate next step.

What do you need from me first? +

A brief summary of the transaction or issue and any key dates or deadlines. Please wait until terms of engagement are agreed before sending confidential documents.

How are fees handled? +

Scope, disbursements, and fees are confirmed clearly in writing before any substantive legal work begins, so there are no unexpected bills.

How long does a conveyancing matter take? +

Timelines depend on the contract terms, financing, and title searches, typically ranging from 30 to 60 days from exchange to settlement. Urgent reviews can be prioritized.

Can the matter be handled by phone and email? +

Yes. Most reviews, negotiations, and settlements are handled efficiently by direct phone call and email, with written summaries sent at each stage.

What if my matter is urgent? +

If a signing deadline, cooling-off period, or settlement date is approaching, call +1 765 239 9619 directly or note the deadline at the top of your inquiry.
